Meeting Preparation Guide

The 10-Point Pre-Meeting IEP Checklist for Families

Written by Guided Path Advocacy Team
Updated September 2026
6 Minute Read

Key Takeaway Summary

  • Request draft copies of the proposed IEP goals at least 5 business days in advance.
  • Prepare a concise 1-page Parent Input Statement highlighting home observations and priorities.
  • Organize documents chronologically so baseline data can be easily referenced during discussion.

Attending an annual Individualized Education Program (IEP) meeting can feel overwhelming without structured preparation. By following this 10-point checklist prior to your meeting date, you can ensure your voice is represented and discussions remain collaborative.

Part 1: Document Gathering

1. Request Draft Goals in Advance

Submit a polite, written request to your case manager asking for draft goal proposals 5 school days prior to the meeting. Reviewing goals at home allows you to note questions without time pressure.

2. Compile Recent Progress Reports

Gather all quarterly progress reports for the current school year. Verify whether goal progress is quantified with data percentages or solely marked with subjective comments.
