How to Write a Concise Parent Concerns Statement That Gets Read

Written by Eleanor Vance, M.Ed.
Published Sept 14, 2026
6 Minute Read

Key Article Takeaways

  • Under IDEA, parent input is an explicit component of the IEP document.
  • Keep your written statement to 1 page using 3 clear sections: Student Strengths, Current Home Observations, and Priority Questions.
  • Submit your statement in writing 3 days before the meeting to be included in the meeting minutes.

One of the most powerful yet underutilized tools in the IEP process is the Parent Concerns Statement. While school evaluators present formal testing metrics, your input provides crucial context about how your child functions outside the structured classroom environment.
