Comprehensive IEP & Educational Consulting

From thorough document audits to pre-meeting agenda structure, we provide families with clear guidance every step of the special education journey.

Core Service

IEP Review

Comprehensive line-by-line review of your child's current IEP or draft document. We analyze goals, present levels, accommodations, and service frequencies to highlight areas for clarification.

Who It May Help:

Parents preparing for annual ARD / IEP reviews, transition plans, or families who feel their child's current goals lack baseline measurement.

What Is Included:

  • Present Levels & Goals audit
  • Accommodations & Related Services review
  • Written review summary report with parent notes

Structured Journey

How We Partner With Families

Our 4-step collaborative consulting framework ensures transparent support from start to finish.

Step 01

Document Intake

We collect current IEPs, evaluation reports, and report cards to conduct an objective background review.

Step 02

Strategy Consultation

1-on-1 prep call to identify core concerns, define measurable goals, and draft your Parent Statement.

Step 03

Meeting Preparation

Formulate a structured pre-meeting agenda and communication protocol for school team discussions.

Step 04

Follow-Up Audit

Review final proposed IEP documents to verify agreed-upon accommodations were accurately included.
