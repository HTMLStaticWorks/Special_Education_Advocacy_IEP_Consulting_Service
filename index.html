Step-by-Step Approach

A Structured Process for Family Support

We break down complex special education workflows into clear, manageable steps. Our process ensures you feel heard, prepared, and focused on your child's needs.

Consulting Notice: Guided Path Advocacy provides educational consulting and advocacy support. Our services do not replace legal, medical, or psychological advice.
Book Consultation
01

Listen & Understand

We take time to listen to your family's experiences, primary educational concerns, and long-term priorities.

02

Review Documentation

Carefully examine current IEPs, 504 plans, evaluations, progress reports, and teacher correspondence.

03

Prepare Agenda & Questions

Construct organized meeting checklists, priority topics, and respectful communication points for the school team.

04

Consult & Support

Provide pre-meeting coaching or direct advocacy support during formal educational consultations.

05

Follow Through

Assist in reviewing post-meeting document drafts, notes, and organizing next steps.

Is Your Child's IEP Audit-Ready?

Check whether your student's special education document contains objective baseline metrics and actionable accommodations.

Criterion 01

Quantifiable Baselines

Are present levels of performance grounded in measurable diagnostic scores rather than subjective teacher notes?

Criterion 02

Targeted Accommodations

Are classroom & testing accommodations specifically tailored for sensory breaks, extra time, or assistive tools?

Criterion 03

Progress Reporting Schedule

Does the plan outline a clear progress report schedule aligned with general education report cards?

Criterion 04

Parent Input Statement

Is your official 1-page parent concerns statement attached and explicitly referenced in Section B?
