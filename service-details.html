Service Overview

An IEP is a complex legal document designed to guide your child's educational support. However, documents often contain vague goal descriptions, outdated baseline data, or missing accommodation specifications. Our IEP Review service provides a thorough, objective analysis to ensure the plan reflects your child's true strengths and educational needs.

Professional Disclaimer: Guided Path Advocacy provides educational consulting and advocacy support. Our services do not replace legal, medical, psychological, or other licensed professional advice.

What We Review in Detail

Present Levels (PLAAFP)

Verifying baseline scores, academic metrics, and teacher observational notes.

Annual Goals & Objectives

Ensuring goals are specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound (SMART).

Deliverables & Takeaways

Following your document review, you will receive:

Written Audit Summary

A section-by-section breakdown with specific questions to ask the school team.

Parent Input Draft

A structured statement of your family's concerns to attach to the official IEP document.
