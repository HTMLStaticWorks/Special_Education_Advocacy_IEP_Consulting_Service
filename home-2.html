Structured Roadmap

The 4-Stage Advocacy Framework

We demystify the special education process by taking families through four proven, milestone-driven steps designed to ensure accountability.

1

Baseline Audit & Record Review

We inspect all past IEP documents, psychological evaluations, and report cards to extract concrete performance metrics.

2

Parent Concerns Statement Formulation

Transform family observations into professional, structured agenda points to be placed directly into school meeting notes.

3

School Meeting Collaboration & Coaching

Pre-meeting rehearsal sessions and strategic support during school conferences to preserve constructive dialogue.

4

Post-Meeting Draft Verification

Verifying that all accommodations, goals, and therapy minutes agreed upon verbally match the final IEP document before signing.
